For You, My Love ❤️

A little piece of my heart

My love,

I don't really know how to start this, because somehow every sentence feels smaller than what I actually want to tell you.

I'm sorry.

I'm sorry because I've realized that somewhere along the way, I made the girl I love question whether I'm truly going to stay. And honestly, that hurts me more than I can explain.

I never wanted my love to become
another reason for you to be afraid. ❤️

I know you're scared that one day I'll leave. I know your past has given you reasons to be scared of trusting someone completely. I can't erase what happened before me, but I can choose how I treat you now.

Tumhe mujh par trust karne ke liye rush karne ki zarurat nahi hai.
Main tumhe sirf words se convince nahi karna chahta. Main chahta hoon ki mere actions tumhe dheere-dheere feel karayein ki you are safe with me. ❤️

I don't want you to feel like you have to constantly check whether I still love you. I don't want you to hide your feelings from me because you're scared I'll judge you or get tired of them.

I want you to be able to tell me when you're scared. When you're overthinking. When something hurts. Even when you don't know exactly how to explain it.

I want my presence to feel like peace,
not another thing you have to worry about.

I know I can't promise that we'll never have problems. I can't promise I'll never make a mistake. But I can promise that I will keep learning, keep listening, and keep trying to love you better.

Because commitment, to me, isn't just saying "I'll never leave."

Commitment mere liye sirf forever bolna nahi hai.
It's choosing you again and again, especially on the days when loving each other takes effort.

So these aren't giant promises.
They're little things I want to give you.

Tumhe perfect banne ki zarurat nahi hai mere liye. You don't have to stop overthinking overnight. You don't have to pretend you're okay when you're not. You don't have to earn my love by becoming someone else.

I love you as you are, and I want to keep learning the person you are becoming too.

And if your heart ever asks,
“What if he leaves?”

I hope you remember:
I'm here. ❤️

I can't control the future. But I can choose what I do today. And today, I choose you.

And tomorrow, I'll try to choose you again.

I'm sorry for making you question something that means so much to me. I'm sorry for the moments where my actions made loving me feel less safe than it should.

Thank you for still being here. Thank you for loving me. And thank you for giving me the chance to do better.

One last thing... 🌷

At the end of every stupid argument, every misunderstanding, every bad day...

It's still you
that I want to come back to. ❤️

You are loved.
You are important to me.
You don't have to be perfect to be loved by me.

And yes... you're still ridiculously cute. 😭❤️

With all my love,
Your idiot, Abir ❤️
